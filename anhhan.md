Becoming an internal leader exposed different resistance and influence dynamics, contributing to his development in organisational development and ontological coaching.

**What this shows:** Ability to make latent ambition sponsorable, build the people-and-process infrastructure to deliver, and recognise institutional and commercial limits. Do not claim sole authorship, confirmed deal-size rankings, or resolution of all internal resistance.
